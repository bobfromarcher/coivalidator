Privacy

COI Validator is a product of de Montfort LLC. We respect your privacy and keep this simple.

What we do with your documents

When you check a certificate, its contents are sent to our analysis service solely to generate your report and are not stored after the report is produced. We do not sell your data.

Basic analytics

We may collect anonymous, aggregate usage data (e.g. number of checks) to operate and improve the service. We use reasonable, industry-standard security including TLS encryption in transit.
