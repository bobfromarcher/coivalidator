Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

de Montfort LLC ("we", "us", "our") operates the COI Validator tool at coivalidator.com (the "Tool"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the Tool.

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Document Content: The text or file you submit for analysis. This content is processed in real time and is not stored after the response is generated.
  • Usage Data: With your consent, we use Google Analytics 4 and Meta Pixel to collect anonymized data about your interaction with the Tool, such as pages visited and time spent. This data helps us improve the Tool.
  • Contact Information: If you email us at hello@coivalidator.com, we collect your email address and any information you provide to respond to your inquiry.

How We Use Document Content

When you submit a Certificate of Insurance for analysis, the content is sent to a third-party AI provider, Fireworks AI, solely to perform the analysis. The document content is processed in real time and is not stored on our servers or by Fireworks AI after the response is delivered. We do not use your document content for any other purpose.

Third-Party AI Processor

We rely on Fireworks AI to analyze the submitted documents. Fireworks AI processes the content only as needed to generate the analysis and does not retain it. We have no control over Fireworks AI's infrastructure, but we have chosen them for their commitment to data privacy.

Cookies and Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 and a Meta Pixel to understand how visitors use the Tool. These services set cookies only after you provide consent via our cookie banner. No cookies are used for essential functionality of the Tool. You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing your browser cookies or adjusting your preferences.

Data Retention

  • Document Content: Not retained; deleted immediately after processing.
  • Analytics Data: Retained according to Google's and Meta's data retention policies. We do not combine this data with personally identifiable information.
  • Contact Emails: Retained as long as needed to address your inquiry, then deleted within a reasonable timeframe unless required for legal purposes.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights under laws like the GDPR or CCPA, including the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Since we do not store document content, there is typically no personal data to access or delete from the Tool itself. If you have contacted us via email, you may request access to or deletion of that correspondence by emailing hello@coivalidator.com. We will respond within the timeframes required by applicable law.

No Sale of Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade any personal data. The Tool is free, and we do not monetize your information.

Payments

The COI Validator Tool is free and does not process payments. No payment information is collected. If you choose to explore TachyonTracker, a paid product by de Montfort LLC, that service has its own privacy policy and may use Stripe for payment processing.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your rights, please email us at hello@coivalidator.com.
